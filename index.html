Projects Highlights

A selection of my research projects and the online business I run.

01.

Chaotic Systems: Dynamics & Mathematical Analysis

Nonlinear Dynamics | Research Project

A theoretical and numerical study of chaotic systems, focusing on their nonlinear dynamics, stability, and characteristic behaviours.
Using the Chua circuit as a case study, I investigate equilibrium points, bifurcations, sensitivity to initial conditions, and the emergence of chaotic attractors through mathematical analysis and numerical simulation.

Python | LTspice | Circuit Prototyping | Experimental Validation
02.

Chaos-Based Encryption & Secure Communications

Signal Synchronization & Communication | Research Project

An exploration of how chaotic dynamics can be leveraged to develop communication systems using synchronized nonlinear oscillators.
Through circuit modeling and simulation, I investigate how information can be encoded into chaotic signals and recovered at the receiver, examining synchronization, signal masking, and the influence of parameter mismatch on transmission reliability.

Python | LTspice | Signal Measurement & Analysis
03.

Digital Product Entrepreneurship & E-commerce Management

Online Store | Digital Printables & Graphic Design

I manage an online business selling digital printables and original designs through e-commerce platforms such as Shopify and Etsy.
